# Support the project

*Data Standardizer* is free for everyone to use — if it adds value to your
project, please consider supporting its ongoing development.

The project is maintained and enhanced by
[@matthew25187](https://github.com/matthew25187) in his personal time and made
available for free for all to use. If you derive a commercial benefit from *Data
Standardizer*, or feel it otherwise adds value to your project, you are asked to
please consider supporting it.

The way to do this is to become a
[GitHub sponsor](https://github.com/sponsors/matthew25187) and make a financial
contribution toward maintenance and enhancement.
